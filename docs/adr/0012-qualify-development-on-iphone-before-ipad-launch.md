# Qualify development on iPhone before iPad launch

When this decision was made, the owner had an iPhone 15 Plus running iOS 26.2.1 and an Instax Mini Link 3 on firmware 0102(RX), with Bluefy 3.9.3, but had not bought the first-release iPad A16 and had only used film. These are the reported versions and must be checked again at trial time. Bluefy runs on iPhone and iPad, but availability of Web Bluetooth does not establish this product's printer or kiosk behavior. Waiting for the iPad blocks integrated development; building print accounting against unproven ejection feedback risks making the wrong outcome contract.

**Trial readiness update, 2026-10-13:** The owner reported that a new fresh-film pack was loaded in the Mini Link 3. At that time, the physical iPhone trial could proceed once its isolated harness and HTTPS setup were ready. This dated update does not mark the development gate as passed or change the separate iPad release gate. See the [issue #17 qualification design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-iphone-print-qualification-design.md) and the [current trial report](../qualification/issue-17-trial-report.md) for observed results and film state.

Use two distinct hardware gates. A fresh-film, end-to-end trial on the iPhone 15 Plus, Bluefy, and Mini Link 3 may unlock the full Admin, Booth Mode, and print-accounting build. Before it passes, only the independent Admin foundation, isolated printer qualification harness, and simulated tests may proceed. Completing the foundation is not a prerequisite for the iPhone gate. The iPhone is a development/test Booth Mode device, not a first-release supported device. The purchased iPad A16 must pass its own end-to-end trial before launch; record its exact model, iPadOS, Bluefy version, and printer firmware then. iPhone success never substitutes for iPad acceptance. Any difference found on the iPad may require changes to shared printer, Booth, or accounting code and another verification pass.

The iPhone trial needs usable fresh film and observed physical output. It must prove the combined camera, restricted navigation, Bluetooth, and printing path, including three consecutive Guest prints, a Retake, allowance exhaustion, disconnect recovery, correct crop/orientation, and reliable feedback for physical film ejection. A command acknowledgement or Film Remaining change alone is insufficient. Ambiguous fault outcomes stay uncertain; if normal completion cannot be distinguished reliably from command acceptance, the gate fails. Used film and simulations can narrow uncertainty but cannot pass this gate. If the iPhone trial fails, the full build remains gated while the approach is reconsidered.

This decision supersedes the **development-timing clause** in [ADR-0005](0005-validate-the-target-booth-browser.md), which required the iPad trial before the full build. It preserves ADR-0005's iPad trial as the release gate and its printer-adapter boundary. The detailed stages are in the [design spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-iphone-qualified-build-design.md).

# Qualify development on iPhone before iPad launch

> **Current scope (2026-10-21):** Original pre-build timing is historical; simulator development and current automatic-completion/iPad gates follow ADR-0025. [ADR-0025](0025-package-accounting-and-booth-recovery.md) and the [replacement contract](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-package-accounting-booth-recovery-design.md) control conflicting behavior. The original decision below is retained as history.

**Historical decision; development timing superseded:** [ADR-0021](0021-move-print-qualification-to-integration-checkpoint.md) replaces this ADR's requirement to pass iPhone qualification before integrated development. Integrated Admin and Booth development may proceed with a simulated printer. The physical-ejection decision and validation remain a checkpoint before printer-backed Booth Mode and Confirmed Print accounting are completed or enabled. The iPad release gate remains in force. The sections below preserve the original decision and trial criteria as historical context; use ADR-0021, the current PRD, and current issues for active instructions.

**Context when this decision was made:** The owner had an iPhone 15 Plus running iOS 26.2.1 and an Instax Mini Link 3 on firmware 0102(RX), with Bluefy 3.9.3, but had not bought the first-release iPad A16 and had only used film. These are the reported versions and must be checked again at trial time. Bluefy runs on iPhone and iPad, but availability of Web Bluetooth does not establish this product's printer or kiosk behavior. Waiting for the iPad was considered a blocker to integrated development; building print accounting against unproven ejection feedback risked making the wrong outcome contract.

**Historical trial readiness update, 2026-10-13:** The owner reported that a new fresh-film pack was loaded in the Mini Link 3. At that time, the physical iPhone trial could proceed once its isolated harness and HTTPS setup were ready. This dated update did not pass the original iPhone gate or change the separate iPad release gate. ADR-0021 now governs development timing. See the [issue #17 qualification design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-iphone-print-qualification-design.md) and the [current trial report](../qualification/issue-17-trial-report.md) for observed results and film state.

**Original development-timing decision, superseded by ADR-0021:** Use two distinct hardware gates. A fresh-film, end-to-end trial on the iPhone 15 Plus, Bluefy, and Mini Link 3 may unlock the full Admin, Booth Mode, and print-accounting build. Before it passes, only the independent Admin foundation, isolated printer qualification harness, and simulated tests may proceed. Completing the foundation is not a prerequisite for the iPhone gate. The iPhone is a development/test Booth Mode device, not a first-release supported device. The purchased iPad A16 must pass its own end-to-end trial before launch; record its exact model, iPadOS, Bluefy version, and printer firmware then. iPhone success never substitutes for iPad acceptance. Any difference found on the iPad may require changes to shared printer, Booth, or accounting code and another verification pass.

**Original iPhone trial criteria (historical evidence target, not the current development gate):** The iPhone trial was intended to use fresh film and observed physical output. It was intended to prove the combined camera, restricted navigation, Bluetooth, and printing path, including three consecutive Guest prints, a Retake, allowance exhaustion, disconnect recovery, correct crop/orientation, and reliable feedback for physical film ejection. A command acknowledgement or Film Remaining change alone is insufficient. Ambiguous fault outcomes stay uncertain; under the original decision, failure to distinguish normal completion from command acceptance failed the gate. Used film and simulations cannot prove physical output or ejection. ADR-0021 now permits integrated development with simulation and retains physical completion as a checkpoint before printer-backed operation is completed or enabled.

This decision supersedes the **development-timing clause** in [ADR-0005](0005-validate-the-target-booth-browser.md), which required the iPad trial before the full build. It preserves ADR-0005's iPad trial as the release gate and its printer-adapter boundary. The detailed stages are in the [design spec](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-iphone-qualified-build-design.md).

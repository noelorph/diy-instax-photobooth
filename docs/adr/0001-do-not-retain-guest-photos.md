# Do not retain guest photos

The booth keeps a Guest photo only in the booth browser's memory for the current capture and print flow, sends it directly to the printer, then discards it. Pausing before a print discards any unprinted photo while keeping its Capture count; resuming begins at welcome. The photo is not uploaded to Next.js or Supabase. Event history retains counts and activity without a photo gallery, email delivery, or QR download. This gives Guests a physical print while limiting exposure of their images; adding digital delivery later would require a new retention decision and consent flow. Direct printing remains subject to the target-device trial in ADR-0005.

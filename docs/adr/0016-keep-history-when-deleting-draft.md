# Keep operational history when deleting a Draft

A Draft may be deleted after all of its Print and Film Reservations are resolved, even if it has a Test Print, Film Stock entry, or other operational record. Deletion removes the Event from Admin lists and erases Client details, Event title, venue, schedule, and Event Media, while retaining an opaque deleted-Event reference for Test Print, stock, and activity records. The app reports deletion complete only after private Event Media is removed. Operational effects on shared Film Stock remain. This lets the Admin remove an unused booking without silently changing physical inventory or erasing the reason for a stock change; it extends the shared-stock and ledger decisions in [ADR-0006](0006-carry-film-stock-across-events.md) and [ADR-0008](0008-record-print-and-stock-changes-transactionally.md).

# Issue #17 — interim iPhone print qualification report

**Recorded:** 2026-10-14; reviewed 2026-10-15, Asia/Manila. **Decision:** development gate remains closed. This is an interim evidence record, not a passing qualification. See the [runbook](issue-17-runbook.md), [approved design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-iphone-print-qualification-design.md), and [issue #17](https://github.com/noelorph/diy-instax-photobooth/issues/17).

## Setup and observed results

The operator reported an iPhone 15 Plus running iOS 26.2.1 and Bluefy 3.9.3, with a Mini Link 3 reporting model `FI033` and firmware `0102`, on continuous power. The fresh pack had eight printable sheets remaining when physical printing began. The no-print checks passed: camera permission denial and Retry camera, camera/capture/Retake/review, crop and orientation, Guided Access, controls, pre-command printer disconnect and reconnect, and non-photo log export.

### Operational findings during setup

- The first iPhone LAN session reached the HTTPS page, but Next.js blocked a cross-origin development resource for the Mac's LAN address. The development configuration was adjusted to allow the current LAN origin; this is a local development setup requirement.
- The initial missing Bluetooth permission prompt was resolved when the operator turned iPhone Bluetooth back on. This was a device setting, not evidence of a printer protocol failure.
- Bluefy did not visibly download the first non-photo log. The qualification page now offers selectable JSON, **Copy JSON**, and **Download JSON**; the operator subsequently exported logs. A silent file download alone is not the verification step.
- The printer was observed to disconnect after being idle. A pre-command reconnect returned the trial to setup and required **Start trial** again; no queued print was sent. A disconnect after a print command may have started remains an uncertain outcome that requires inspection.
- The operator initially found the text-like controls and setup camera preview difficult to use. The qualification page controls and setup preview were adjusted in this issue branch; the operator later reported the page controls, preview, crop, and orientation working. These interface checks do not establish the ejection signal.

Seven qualification sheets physically printed. The operator confirmed that the physical sheets matched the reviewed photo crop and orientation. One additional sheet printed through the official Instax app for Bluetooth diagnosis, moving the printer count from eight to **zero** overall. The official-app sheet is not a Guest-flow qualification print. Six qualification attempts have exported timelines; the second physical sheet's attempt did not have a full exported timeline. That earlier second attempt became **Outcome uncertain** after a sheet emerged because the operator did not tap **Observed film ejection** before the observation timeout; physical inspection and reconciliation resolved that run. Sheet 6 also timed out because its final confirmation was missed. Sheet 7 was manually confirmed within its timeout and needs no reconciliation. Page reloads and reconciliation interrupted runs. No single run has yet demonstrated three consecutive prints and zero local allowance.

## Official-app diagnostic print

The operator captured one official Instax app print in `link3-official-print-1.pklg`. The trace spans more than 60 seconds after the sheet fully emerged, but PacketLogger was briefly paused after emergence. The trace has a complete image upload and print command; the image-bearing capture stays outside the repository. Review of the separate phone video confirms the official app leaves photo review just after 00:50, a sheet begins emerging around 01:06, and the sheet is fully out around 01:11. The printer subsequently reported two sheets. The video has no usable recording timestamp, and the PacketLogger text on the Mac screen is too small to read; its elapsed marks cannot be aligned precisely to the trace clock. The video also shows the operator using the Mac shortly after emergence, but does not identify the exact pause boundary.

Relative to the trace's image-upload start, the printer received the execute-print command at +5.110 s and acknowledged it at +5.138 s. A polled `0x0002` printer-function response then changed from `00023300000b00000000` to `000233127f1000000000` at +6.006 s. The response still contained `12 7f` when film count fell 3 → 2 at +19.223 s (`000232127f0b00000000`). At +20.108 s, a later response became `00023200000b00000000`; subsequent responses remained at count two with that field cleared. PacketLogger has a 7.887-second gap from trace-clock 17:50:07.506 to 17:50:15.393, beginning about 3.8 seconds after that field cleared. The gap does not hide the command, count drop, or field transition, but an event during the pause would not be recorded. The `12 7f` field is a **candidate activity signal**, distinct in time from the film-count change, but its meaning and ordering against complete physical emergence are unverified. No separate Link 3 status notification was observed in the recorded intervals of the official-app trace. The earlier no-print trace lacked the execute-print command and this `12 7f` sequence.

## No-film readiness review

The current harness requests raw `0x0002` status every **900 ms**, serializing requests so another does not start while a prior request is in flight. Actual response gaps can be longer, as sheet 7's 1.320-second gap shows. The official-app trace's count-to-field separation was only 0.885 s; Bluefy polling can therefore sample both changes together. Polling faster might narrow gaps but has not been checked against BLE transfer contention, and no polling rate alone proves that a status transition always follows complete physical emergence. The page keeps status diagnostic only and requires a separate manual confirmation.

The existing simulated qualification tests passed on 2026-10-15: 35 tests across the printer session, local trial controller, and qualification page. They cover raw status display/logging without automatic confirmation, bounded observation, the ten-second post-marker tail, uncertainty handling, and three manual observations exhausting the local allowance with a blocked fourth command. Simulation cannot establish physical timing or printer semantics. No printer or film was used in this review.

**Next step:** use the [runbook](issue-17-runbook.md) to prepare synchronized evidence before loading more film. A future isolated diagnostic print would require **one sheet in addition to at least three sheets** reserved for a separate uninterrupted qualification run. The field must be distinguished from film count and shown never to clear before complete emergence across the qualifying prints. If that cannot be established, keep the full build gated and reconsider the printer/browser approach under the existing product decision.

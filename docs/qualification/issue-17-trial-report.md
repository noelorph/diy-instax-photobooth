# Issue #17 — interim iPhone print qualification report

**Recorded:** 2026-10-14, Asia/Manila. **Decision:** development gate remains closed. This is an interim evidence record, not a passing qualification. See the [runbook](issue-17-runbook.md), [approved design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-iphone-print-qualification-design.md), and [issue #17](https://github.com/noelorph/diy-instax-photobooth/issues/17).

## Setup and observed results

The operator reported an iPhone 15 Plus running iOS 26.2.1 and Bluefy 3.9.3, with a Mini Link 3 reporting model `FI033` and firmware `0102`, on continuous power. The fresh pack had eight printable sheets remaining when physical printing began. The no-print checks passed: camera permission denial and Retry camera, camera/capture/Retake/review, crop and orientation, Guided Access, controls, pre-command printer disconnect and reconnect, and non-photo log export.

Six qualification sheets physically printed and matched the reviewed photo crop and orientation. One additional sheet printed through the official Instax app for Bluetooth diagnosis, moving the printer count from eight to one overall. The official-app sheet is not a Guest-flow qualification print. Five qualification attempts have exported timelines; the second physical sheet's attempt did not have a full exported timeline. An earlier second attempt became **Outcome uncertain** after a sheet emerged because the operator did not tap **Observed film ejection** before the observation timeout; physical inspection and reconciliation resolved that run. The latest attempt also timed out after the operator marked full emergence but missed the separate final confirmation. The video, printer count, and operator inspection establish that this sheet printed correctly; the software outcome remains uncertain until physical reconciliation. Page reloads and reconciliation interrupted runs. No single run has yet demonstrated three consecutive prints and zero local allowance.

The standard status responses during sheet 5 changed from `0002b4127f0b00000000` to `0002b300000b00000000` when film count dropped 4 → 3. This was 57.169 s after Print and 14.399 s after command acknowledgement. The video records full emergence at approximately 57 s after Print; its whole-second marks cannot establish subsecond ordering. For sheet 1, the status change was 45.618 s after Print, while the operator timed full emergence at about 46.99 s. The candidate status therefore may precede complete ejection. Similar status transitions accompanied the other logged prints. These responses have only been observed together with film-count changes, so they have not established a distinct, reliable physical-ejection signal.

The optional Mini Link 3 status characteristic was subscribed successfully before sheet 5 (`link3-status-listening` at 08:23:44.133 UTC), but **no `link3-status` notification** appeared during upload, command acknowledgement, or physical ejection. The preceding no-print run also subscribed successfully and received none. The first seven events in the latest exported JSON belong to the preceding run `run-de542e1c-6cbe-462e-af87-7399af1d2d4e`; each event's own `runId` identifies its run. The [public reverse-engineered protocol notes](https://github.com/dgwilson/ESP32-Instax-Bridge/blob/main/INSTAX_PROTOCOL.md#link-3-specific-services) identify the characteristic as Notify, but do not document an ejection notification from it.

Sheet 6 was recorded with the printer and Bluefy screen in the same video. The status response remained `000232127f0b00000000` with film count two through 12:41:56.248 UTC. The next sampled response, at 12:41:56.938, was `00023100000b00000000`: the count fell to one and `12 7f` cleared in the **same sample**, 55.983 s after Print and 14.549 s after acknowledgement. Video shows the sheet still emerging near 02:12 and fully outside around 02:14, approximately 56 s after the 01:18 Print tap. The operator tapped **Sheet fully emerged** at 12:41:57.989, 1.051 s after the changed response. Neither the video timestamps nor this polling interval resolve whether the candidate field cleared before or after complete emergence, and this run does not distinguish it from the film-count change. No `link3-status` notification was logged. The app recorded **Outcome uncertain** at 12:42:42.392 because the separate **Observed film ejection** confirmation was never tapped; this timeout does not reverse the physical print evidence. The status-tail text was mistaken for a control, so the page and runbook now make that final action explicit.

## Official-app diagnostic print

The operator captured one official Instax app print in `link3-official-print-1.pklg`. The trace spans more than 60 seconds after the sheet fully emerged, but PacketLogger was briefly paused after emergence. The trace has a complete image upload and print command; the image-bearing capture stays outside the repository. Review of the separate phone video confirms the official app leaves photo review just after 00:50, a sheet begins emerging around 01:06, and the sheet is fully out around 01:11. The printer subsequently reported two sheets. The video has no usable recording timestamp, and the PacketLogger text on the Mac screen is too small to read; its elapsed marks cannot be aligned precisely to the trace clock. The video also shows the operator using the Mac shortly after emergence, but does not identify the exact pause boundary.

Relative to the trace's image-upload start, the printer received the execute-print command at +5.110 s and acknowledged it at +5.138 s. A polled `0x0002` printer-function response then changed from `00023300000b00000000` to `000233127f1000000000` at +6.006 s. The response still contained `12 7f` when film count fell 3 → 2 at +19.223 s (`000232127f0b00000000`). At +20.108 s, a later response became `00023200000b00000000`; subsequent responses remained at count two with that field cleared. PacketLogger has a 7.887-second gap from trace-clock 17:50:07.506 to 17:50:15.393, beginning about 3.8 seconds after that field cleared. The gap does not hide the command, count drop, or field transition, but an event during the pause would not be recorded. The `12 7f` field is a **candidate activity signal**, distinct in time from the film-count change, but its meaning and ordering against complete physical emergence are unverified. No separate Link 3 status notification was observed in the recorded intervals of the official-app trace. The earlier no-print trace lacked the execute-print command and this `12 7f` sequence.

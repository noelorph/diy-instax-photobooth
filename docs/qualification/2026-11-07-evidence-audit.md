## Remaining safe error/interruption recovery

The existing contract requires an ambiguous accepted attempt to remain Pending, with no automatic resend and no new printing while job safety is unknown. Reload, idle status, a positive count and reconnect alone do not resolve the attempt. The official app's local `COMPLETED` state or progress 100 can accompany an error; neither is sufficient success evidence.

Checklist P3/P4/P10/P11 cover error classification or the approved inspection fallback, correct outcome resolution, reload and old-job clearance. P6–P9 cover Bluetooth, internet, page/journal and camera interruptions; P14 covers lifecycle/stop races and overlapping faults. F6 integration remains open. A qualified generic-fault/inspection procedure is an allowed route; an exclusive no-film classifier is not mandatory.

The recovery report's power-cycle/reload/different-image sequence is a proposed experiment only. No cancellation guarantee or safe immediate-resume procedure has been proven. Accepted normal detection results remain accepted while these separate checks are outstanding. This audit changes no product decision or release gate.
